* HOW DO I CANCEL MY OFFER TO PURCHASE?

Each purchase agreement has specific cancellation clauses. Typically, you can cancel within a defined inspection period. Please review your contract details or contact your agent immediately for guidance on legal processes and potential financial implications.

* HOW WILL YOU REFUND MY ESCROW DEPOSIT?

Refunds of the escrow deposit are managed by the closing attorney or title company, following the terms of the purchase contract. If the sale is canceled according to the contract's contingency clauses, the funds are typically returned via wire transfer within 5-10 business days.

* WHAT TYPES OF PROPERTIES DO YOU SPECIALIZE IN?
